# Precut 2.7.1

- Introduce a rounded, resizable sequence editor with transcript on the left, video in the center, a contextual inspector on the right, and Video / Audio / Captions / Text tracks below. Light and dark themes adapt down to 1080 × 720.
- Arrange multiple recordings, reuse a source, split, trim, duplicate and ripple-delete clips with linked audio and captions. Snap to clip and word boundaries, zoom around the pointer, and fit or scroll long timelines.
- Correct Thai/English words inline with IME-safe Enter and Escape. Cut short words independently using the inline trash button, clear-and-Enter, or Delete / Backspace; each cut is reversible in one Undo step. Compact inputs and subtle pointer focus avoid oversized outlines.
- Add six offline HyperFrames text designs with editable content, bundled fonts, color, placement and timing, while retaining all 17 original caption animations and six classic presets. Apply caption changes to a cue, clip or project, and preview or cancel design trials.
- Add Dissolve, Fade black, Slide, Wipe, Zoom and Whip at clip joins. Picture-only transitions preserve speech timing and timeline length, validate available source frames and share rendering between cached preview and MP4 export.
- Preserve source transcripts, translations and corrections across reused clips. Back up legacy projects before sequence migration, retain persistent Undo/Redo, and keep clip-specific cuts and decoration separate.
- Export the complete edit to MP4 or remapped caption text to SRT. Explain unsupported XML combinations instead of producing an incomplete edit. Improve linked external audio offsets, interrupted-worker recovery and long-recording viewport performance.

- Stabilize inline editing immediately after changing clips, and make native release acceptance reproducible on clean macOS and Windows checkouts.

Downloads, editing, preview and project storage remain free. Exports retain production license enforcement. All templates, fonts and media processing remain offline. Unsigned installers use manual installation and updates. Apple Silicon requires macOS 27 or later; Intel requires macOS 15 or later. This release targets Mac Apple Silicon, Mac Intel and Windows x64, with native and packaged acceptance required before Store publication.
